FAQ

Is there a minimum order?

No. Order a single puck or a few hundred — there's no minimum quantity.

What kind of pucks are these?

Regulation 3"×1", 6 oz vulcanized rubber hockey pucks. Great for games, gifts, trophies, team swag, or display.

Do I have to provide finished artwork?

Not at all. Pick one of our designs, upload your own art, or just describe your idea and we'll design it for you. You approve a free proof before paying.

What files can I upload?

PNG, JPG, SVG, or PDF up to 10 MB. Higher resolution is better — aim for at least 1000×1000 px for photos.

How long does it take?

Once you approve the proof, pucks typically ship in 5–7 business days.
